its assets retain their upstream authorship and notices.

the licenses and copyright notices of those files; the repository's default
license does not replace upstream licenses.

| Krita port patch | KDE/krita at `3a9b5ef03f8312b6f630fc5d475e1692598e8719`; file-specific GPL, LGPL, BSD and other upstream notices |
| KDE Frameworks patches | KDE 6.18.0 sources; LGPL and BSD notices in the affected files |

`LICENSES/`. Dependencies and Qt are downloaded by build scripts rather than
vendored in this repository; their source distributions contain their notices.

Application bundles, Qt libraries, fonts, brush bundles, signing material and
generated IPA files are excluded from this source repository.
